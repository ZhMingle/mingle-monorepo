# Search Marketing curriculum · 2026-11-26

The provided role outline is a learning input, not a verified Webzilla job description. Six paths retain its ten modules while grouping shared foundations and tools with the work they support.

| Path | Supplied modules | Topics |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Digital Marketing Fundamentals & Metrics | 01, 02 | 19 |
| Google Ads | 03 | 15 |
| SEO, Search Console & Research Tools | 04, 07, 08 | 44 |
| GA4 & GTM — Measurement and Tracking | 05, 06 | 24 |
| Ecommerce Marketing & CRO | 09 | 10 |
| AI Search & Marketing Reporting | 10 | 11 |

Study fundamentals first, Ads and SEO in parallel, then measurement, ecommerce and reporting. Development knowledge helps with technical SEO, rendering, performance and data-layer contracts. Prioritize marketing decisions, paid search, SEO strategy and profitable conversion.

Each topic has bilingual goals, a concrete self-check, a reference and a stage project. Generated lessons use scenario choices and written business cases, calculations or configuration descriptions. The hidden assessment contract requires a concrete rubric, a primary case, a changed-constraint transfer case and correct/incorrect regression fixtures. Independent evidence and delayed retention rules remain separate from practice progress.

Sources reviewed for curriculum structure:

- [Google Skillshop](https://skillshop.withgoogle.com/): Ads and Analytics learning.
- [Search Central SEO guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): organic discovery and useful content.
- [Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): ordinary SEO remains relevant; no special AI markup guarantees inclusion.
- [GA4 developer guidance](https://developers.google.com/analytics/devguides/collection/ga4) and [GTM guidance](https://developers.google.com/tag-platform/tag-manager): events, implementation and verification.
- [Search Console performance guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553): query/page scope and metrics.
- [Semrush Academy](https://www.semrush.com/academy/) and [Ahrefs Academy](https://ahrefs.com/academy): vendor learning and research workflows.
- [Shopify SEO help](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/seo): ecommerce SEO. The supplied Academy URL redirects; [Shopify's Academy notice](https://help.shopify.com/en/partners/help-support/faq/academy) says Academy ended on 2026-10-29, so the UI links to current help instead.
- [Firebase experiment concepts](https://firebase.google.com/docs/ab-testing/ab-concepts): experiment design and outcome interpretation; exercises do not require Firebase or claim a live experiment ran.

Changing product interfaces and policies should be checked in the linked documentation when applying a plan. Lessons use explicit assumptions rather than inventing current settings, forecasts or campaign outcomes.
